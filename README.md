# Attempt Tracker

<img src="icon.png" alt="Fish and stopwatch" width="48" height="48">

A simple fishing tracker that shows:

- Successful catches, estimated failed attempts, and catch rate.
- Automatically detected shark-lure use, including when no lures are available.
- Logged-in time and active fishing time.
- Saved sessions, a manual reset button, history, and CSV export.

Fishing time pauses when you stop fishing, log out, fill your inventory, or the spot moves. Session totals stay saved until you reset them.

Tracks qualified tick-manipulated fishing from observed timer actions, including supported two-tick harpooning and three-tick methods. Catch rates use supported attempts; failed catches are inferred.
